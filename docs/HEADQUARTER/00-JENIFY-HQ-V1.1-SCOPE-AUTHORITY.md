# JENIFY HQ V1.1 - Scope Authority

**Status:** Authoritative scope correction overlay  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Applies to:** `JENIFY-HQ-BLUEPRINT-ARCHITECTURE.md`, `JENIFY-HQ-IMPLEMENTATION-MASTER-PLAN.md`, `JENIFY-HQ-CURRENT-STATUS.md`

This file records the Founder-approved scope correction made after reviewing the original HQ blueprint. It **supersedes any conflicting wording** in the Version 1.0 canonical documents until the consolidated Version 1.1 files replace them in-repo.

## Canonical Rule

> **Jenify HQ is the command brain + creation machine of Jenify Labs. HQ manages Jenify Labs' work and the creation/lifecycle of Jenify products. Dedicated products perform the outside-world operational service they were built to provide.**

Kid-simple version: **HQ is the factory that makes and improves the tools. The tools do the outside-world jobs.**

## Hard Creation vs Operation Boundary

HQ owns and coordinates:
- Founder commands, missions, projects and tasks
- research and R&D
- product planning and design
- software, website, AI-system, firmware and hardware-product creation
- testing, QA and cybersecurity review
- release preparation and approved deployment
- product maintenance/improvement missions
- AI-worker coordination
- evidence, approvals, documentation and company memory
- mission/project/AI budgets and cost visibility

Dedicated products own their domain operation after release. Examples:
- a factory-monitoring product monitors factories
- a vehicle-tracking product tracks vehicles
- a generator-monitoring product monitors generators
- a CRM product handles CRM work
- a warehouse product manages warehouse operations

If Jenify needs a new operational capability, HQ should create or improve the dedicated product that provides it. HQ must not absorb the operational service into itself.

## Corrections Across the Blueprint

### Maintenance & Operations
Means maintaining Jenify-created software/services, releases, incidents and internal platform health. It does **not** mean operating customer factories, fleets, warehouses or other domain businesses.

### Finance
Means mission budgets, project costs, AI/provider spending and approval visibility. It is **not** bookkeeping, payroll or full accounting.

### Business & Client Support
Means requirements, delivery coordination, important client communication and handoff. It is **not** a full CRM or helpdesk.

### World / Network Map and 3D HQ UI
May visualize missions, products, deployments, development connections, AI workers and high-level product-health evidence. It must **not** silently become the operational control dashboard for a customer's factory, fleet or device network.

### External Action Gateway
Controls sensitive actions required for Jenify Labs work: code changes, builds, tests, documents, cloud changes, approved releases and controlled development/test hardware actions. It is **not** a generic remote-control layer for customer business operations.

### Hardware and Devices
HQ may use lab/test devices, firmware flashing, simulation and controlled telemetry while creating or verifying hardware products. Permanent operational device/fleet control belongs in the dedicated product built for that purpose.

### Phase 16
The correct phase name and intent is:

> **Phase 16 - Product + Hardware Creation & Lifecycle**

Goal: extend HQ's Product Factory so HQ can research, design, prototype, build, test, release and improve Jenify software/hardware products. HQ does not become the runtime operational controller for those products' customer-domain jobs.

### Phase 17
Advanced autonomy means HQ can autonomously handle more of **Jenify Labs' creation and product-lifecycle work** inside authority. It does not mean HQ autonomously operates customer businesses or physical systems as a universal control platform.

## Permanent Scope Guard for All 17 Phases

For every phase, ask:

1. Does this capability help Jenify Labs **think, create, coordinate, verify, release, maintain or remember its work/products**? If yes, it may belong in HQ.
2. Does this capability mainly perform the **customer/domain operational service itself**? If yes, build it as a separate Jenify product and let HQ create/manage its lifecycle.

No later phase may silently override this boundary.
