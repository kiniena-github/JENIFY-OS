# Jenify Labs Universal Creation System — Independent Research Brief

Research-only. Do not deploy or modify production behavior.

## Goal
Design a practical architecture that lets a small company create nearly anything code-driven with zero additional recurring cost first, then compare very-cheap and budget tiers only when free routes are not practical.

## Required capability coverage
- Desktop apps
- Mobile apps
- Websites
- SaaS / platforms / general software
- Hardware / IoT
- Robotics
- Games
- 3D / simulation
- Multimedia
- Serious studio-quality movies, animation, VFX
- Songs, music, audio, voice and sound design
- AI media pipelines
- Testing, automation, deployment, orchestration
- Any other major code-driven capability that materially expands what the company can build

## Hard constraints
- No separately billed AI APIs.
- Existing subscriptions should be maximized before adding spend: ChatGPT Plus, Claude Max 20x, Google AI Pro.
- Prefer open-source and commercially usable tools/models.
- Explicitly flag licensing/commercial-use restrictions.
- For hardware/robotics, distinguish free simulation/prototyping from unavoidable physical component/manufacturing costs.
- Separate three strategies: FREE, VERY CHEAP, BUDGET.
- Include recommended stack, local vs cloud placement, on-demand GPU approach, storage, deployment, automation, media pipeline, hardware/robotics pipeline and fallbacks.
- Avoid vague lists: identify what is actually worth standardizing on.
- Use current pricing/license facts only when verifiable; do not guess.

## Independent reviewer assignment
Act as an independent technical architect/reviewer. Do not merely approve the brief. Produce your own recommended architecture and challenge weak assumptions.

Return:
1. Your FREE strategy.
2. Your VERY CHEAP strategy.
3. Your BUDGET strategy.
4. Estimated variable monthly costs and what drives them.
5. The five most important architectural principles/strategies.
6. Major limitations or things that still require real-world money.
7. Where you disagree with the obvious/simple approach and why.

Keep the result concise enough to compare against four other independent recommendations, but comprehensive enough to make a company-level decision.
