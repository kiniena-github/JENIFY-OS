# Jenify Labs AI Corporate Structure V0

Status: **DRAFT FOR INDEPENDENT CROSS-MODEL REVIEW — NOT FOUNDER-APPROVED**

This document is intentionally a V0 proposal. Claude, Gemini, Jules, and Codex must review the exact same proposal independently before the Founder decides the final structure.

## 1. Design goal

Build a corporate-level AI operating structure for Jenify Labs that uses the useful capabilities already included in the current subscriptions with minimal waste, while preserving strong review, security, product governance, and Founder control.

Current monthly baseline supplied by Founder:
- Claude Max: **$200/month**
- ChatGPT Plus: **$20/month**
- Google AI Pro: **$20/month**
- Current total: **$240/month**

Future scenarios to evaluate, only if evidence justifies changing plans:
- rebalance roughly toward **$100 Claude + $100 ChatGPT + $20 Google** if an appropriate ChatGPT plan/credit configuration is available at decision time;
- expand roughly toward **$200 Claude + $100 ChatGPT + $20 Google** only if both engineering ecosystems are measurably saturated and productive.

Do not recommend upgrades merely because more quota is better. Recommend changes only from observed bottlenecks, queueing, repeated limit hits, or measurable output gains.

## 2. Governance principles

1. Founder remains final authority for business direction, paid services, production deployment, destructive migrations, compliance commitments, irreversible decisions, and promotion of an idea/product on the product-status ladder.
2. Product status must always be explicit: **CURRENT PRODUCT / IN DEVELOPMENT / PROTOTYPE / R&D-EXPERIMENT / FUTURE IDEA / SUPERSEDED-OLD**.
3. An AI researching or prototyping an idea never automatically turns it into an existing Jenify Labs product.
4. The company may define up to roughly 40 specialist roles, but all roles are **not** expected to run simultaneously. Mission teams should activate only the capabilities needed for the task.
5. Independent verification should cross model/vendor boundaries when practical. A model should not be the sole auditor of its own high-impact work.
6. GitHub is the live task/code/evidence bus. Google Drive is the long-term archive/knowledge layer.
7. No extra paid API or cloud service is activated without Founder approval. Prefer included subscription capacity where practical.
8. Do not burn quota just to say every feature is being used. Every useful capability should have a defined job, but activation must be demand-driven.
9. Product-specific engineering pods may differ. Jenify OS, Jenify Studio, Jenify News, Jenify Quick Editor, and future hardware/R&D projects should not be forced into one identical specialist mix.
10. Humans remain necessary for physical-world validation, customer interviews, factory-floor usability, legal sign-off, and other real-world evidence that an AI cannot actually observe.

## 3. Leadership model

Founder sits above a five-role AI Leadership Council. The council does **not** make decisions by simple 3-vs-2 voting. Leadership authority depends on task domain and evidence.

- Strategy/product question: ChatGPT leads; Claude and Gemini challenge.
- Engineering question: Claude CTO leads; OpenAI/Google engineering lanes provide independent review when useful.
- Research/market/regulation question: Gemini Research Chief leads; ChatGPT evaluates business meaning; Claude checks technical feasibility.
- Material unresolved disagreement: evidence is compared and escalated to Founder when it becomes a real business/product/money/legal decision.

## 7. Automation target

Desired experience:
1. Founder or ChatGPT HQ creates one routed task.
2. Dispatcher activates only relevant lanes/specialists.
3. Claude, Gemini, Jules and/or Codex work independently where appropriate.
4. Results/evidence return to the same GitHub task or PR.
5. Mechanical CI/tests run automatically.
6. Cross-model audit compares material disagreements.
7. Founder is interrupted only for genuine Founder gates or unresolved business choices.

No model may claim a worker is automated unless its actual trigger, authentication, permissions, result handoff, and failure handling have been tested.

## 8. Budget/resource review questions

Review all roles against the current **$200 Claude + $20 ChatGPT + $20 Google** baseline and the possible future rebalancing scenarios.

Answer:
1. Which platform is most likely to be the real bottleneck under this roster?
2. Are we over-allocating roles to a platform whose paid quota cannot realistically support them?
3. Are we leaving useful included capacity unused?
4. Which tasks should move from Claude to Codex/Jules/Antigravity, if any?
5. Which research should be single-model versus cross-model?
6. What measurable triggers should cause a subscription downgrade, rebalance, or upgrade?
7. Can the current $240/month structure support this organization without extra paid APIs?

## 9. Required independent-review format

Each reviewer must challenge V0 independently. Do not copy another model's conclusion first.

For every material role or structural element, use one of:
- **KEEP**
- **ADD**
- **REMOVE**
- **MERGE**
- **MOVE**
- **CHANGE AUTHORITY**
- **CHANGE ACTIVATION**

Then provide:
1. Overall verdict.
2. Strongest parts of V0.
3. Biggest structural mistakes or duplicated roles.
4. Missing capabilities.
5. Which items are real agents/workers versus merely tools/interfaces.
6. Recommended final roster (maximum 40 roles; fewer is allowed and preferred if stronger).
7. Leadership/hierarchy changes.
8. Automation and communication design.
9. Review/quality/security gates.
10. Subscription/budget recommendation for now and objective triggers for future plan changes.
11. Top ten changes to make before Founder approval.
